Fair questions

Asked by every smart owner

Will my customers know it's an AI?

It sounds natural and most callers just get helped. But if someone asks, it answers honestly — it's your shop's AI assistant. In our experience customers care about one thing: someone picked up during the rush and handled it. Being straight about it is exactly why they trust it.

What happens on a call it can't handle?

It takes the caller's details, tells them you'll call back personally, and texts you immediately marked URGENT. It never guesses, never quotes prices you didn't approve, and never argues with anyone.

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a site?

Facebook shows your page to people who already found you, wrapped in ads for your competitors. A website is yours: your menu, your number, your reviews — first thing on Google when someone nearby searches for what you do.

You send one text. That's the whole process — no contracts, no fees, no phone-tree runaround. If you built a website with us, it's yours; we'll help you move it wherever you want.
